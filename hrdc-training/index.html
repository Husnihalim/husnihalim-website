03 Common Questions

Before You Book

Is every program 100% HRDC claimable?
Yes. All programs are delivered by an HRDC Accredited Trainer and are fully claimable under the SBL-Khas scheme. We provide all documentation required for your grant application and claim submission — you focus on the training, we handle the paperwork.
Can the program be customized to our industry?
Always. Every program is built around your context — your industry, your machines, your processes, and your specific pain points. No generic slides. Case studies and exercises will reference your actual operations where possible. Semiconductor, automotive, aerospace, FMCG, oil & gas — all sectors have been covered.
What is the minimum group size?
Typically 10–25 participants per session. In-house delivery at your facility is strongly preferred — participants learn significantly better in their own production environment, and exercises can be conducted on the actual shop floor.
Do you deliver training outside Klang Valley?
Yes — programs have been delivered across Peninsular Malaysia including Penang, Johor, Perak, and Pahang. Travel arrangements are factored into the program proposal upfront, with no surprises.
How far in advance should we book?
3–4 weeks ahead is ideal — to allow for content customization, a pre-training needs analysis call, and HRDC grant application lead time. Urgent bookings within 2 weeks can sometimes be accommodated depending on schedule availability.
