Impact: HighEffort: 5 minutesNo dependencies

2. Run PageSpeed Insights and fix any LCP issues

Visit pagespeed.web.dev and test husnihalim.com. If LCP > 2.5s, compress hero images with squoosh.app and add loading="lazy" to below-fold images. Static Netlify sites typically score 90+ but images are the usual culprit.

Impact: HighEffort: 30–60 minNo dependencies

3. Add og:image to all pages missing it

Create one branded share image (1200×630px) — your headshot + logo + tagline. Add <meta property="og:image" content="https://husnihalim.com/assets/og-default.jpg"> to the ~13 pages missing it. This improves click-through when links are shared on LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

Impact: MediumEffort: 1–2 hrsNeeds: design share image first

4. Add internal cross-links from blog posts to new pages

In the existing 5 blog posts, add inline contextual links to: /case-studies/ (e.g. "real-world results"), /industries/automotive/, /hrdc-training/ (when training is mentioned). This passes PageRank to the new pages and helps Google discover them faster.

Impact: MediumEffort: 1 hrNo dependencies

5. Verify robots.txt is not blocking Googlebot

Visit husnihalim.com/robots.txt and confirm it allows crawling. Netlify may not auto-create one — if it's missing, add a robots.txt at root: "User-agent: * Allow: /" with a Sitemap: line pointing to your sitemap.

Impact: CriticalEffort: 10 minNo dependencies
