05Common Questions

Before You Book

How is this different from a standard 5S awareness training?
Most 5S awareness programs teach the definitions and leave. This program focuses specifically on implementation and sustainability — building audit systems, writing visual standards, and creating the accountability structures that prevent the usual 6-month fade. The Carotino consulting framework is built into the delivery.
Can 5S training be done in one day?
Yes — a condensed 1-day version covering Sort, Set-in-Order, Shine, and the audit framework is available. The 2-day version adds deeper implementation work on Standardize and Sustain, and includes a full audit system design workshop. The recommendation depends on your team's current 5S maturity.
Should managers attend 5S training alongside operators?
Strongly recommended. 5S fails most often due to management not modelling and enforcing the standard — not because operators don't know how to do it. The program includes specific content on leadership behaviours and management's role in sustaining 5S.
Can the 5S program be combined with safety or QMS training?
Yes — 5S connects naturally with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and general safety housekeeping requirements. Content can be adjusted to explicitly map 5S activities to your existing QMS or safety management framework.
Is this program 100% HRDC claimable?
Yes. Fully claimable under SBL-Khas with complete documentation support for your grant application and claim submission.
