Malaysian manufacturers face real competitive pressure from automation and AI adoption — but most AI training is filled with jargon and theory disconnected from the factory floor. This program is different: practical, clear, and focused on what AI and Industry 4.0 actually mean for production operations. Participants assess their own Industry 4.0 readiness, understand real use cases from comparable manufacturers, and leave with a realistic, phased digital transformation roadmap — not a buzzword deck.

05Common Questions

Before You Book

Do participants need a technical background to attend?
No. This program is designed for mixed audiences — engineers, operations managers, and senior leadership. Technical concepts are explained clearly without assuming a software or data science background. The focus is on decision-making and practical application, not programming.
Is this program relevant for non-manufacturing organizations?
Yes — it was delivered to JPA (Jabatan Perkhidmatan Awam) in 2025 for a government-sector audience. The core principles of AI readiness and digital transformation apply across industries. Content is adjusted based on your sector.
How does this program relate to our HRDC levy? Is AI training claimable?
Yes — AI and digital skills training has been an explicit priority area for HRD Corp grant allocation in recent years under the Future Skills initiatives. This program is fully claimable under SBL-Khas.
Can the program go deeper on specific technologies like computer vision or predictive maintenance?
Yes — a customized version can focus on specific technologies relevant to your production environment (e.g., vision-based quality inspection for electronics manufacturers, predictive maintenance for heavy equipment). Scope is agreed during the pre-training consultation.
Is this program 100% HRDC claimable?
Yes. Delivered by an HRDC Accredited Trainer and fully claimable under SBL-Khas. Full documentation support provided.
