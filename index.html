01 Profile

About Husni Halim

Husni Halim is a seasoned manufacturing professional, Principal Consultant, and accredited HRDC trainer with over 16 years of hands-on experience in heavy manufacturing. Specializing in the High Voltage (HV) Power Cable and Oil & Gas sectors, Husni’s career is defined by a global perspective on operational excellence.

A University of Arizona graduate (B.Sc. Aerospace Engineering, 2003), Husni spent more than four years based in Oslo, Norway, working with a premier Norwegian oil and gas engineering firm. Prior to his tenure in Scandinavia, he served as a Process Kaizen Engineer for the world's leading power cable manufacturer, spearheading Kaizen initiatives at major global sites including Sorocaba (Brazil), Tianjin (China), Cikampek (Indonesia), Napoli (Italy) and many others. As the Managing Director of Visi Armada Consulting, Husni focuses on transforming factory operations and elevating workforce capabilities. His proven methodology integrates high-impact tools to drive efficiency and productivity, including, Kaizen Methodologies & Cultural Transformation,Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) & TPM,Lean Manufacturing & 5S Implementation. Husni’s track record of driving national industrial growth is highlighted by his role as Lead Consultant for TERAJU’s Pritech 4.0 initiative, where he successfully oversaw OEE implementation across 30 Bumiputera factories throughout Malaysia. Building on this momentum, his recent and upcoming projects focus on high-level process optimization and strategic streamlining for Pos Logistics Berhad: Serving as Lead Facilitator for Business Process Mapping sessions to streamline complex logistics operations. Returning to his aerospace roots, Husni is leading a year longMAB Engineering Services Sdn Bhd (MABES) designed to enhance aviation maintenance efficiency and safety standards.
