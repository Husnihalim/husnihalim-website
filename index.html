Why Kaizen?

Kaizen—continuous improvement embedded in daily operations—is the difference between reactive firefighting and systematic, sustained improvement. Organizations that embed this methodology see measurable results within weeks.

Investment Returns: Typical kaizen projects eliminate RM 50,000–80,000 in annual waste (scrap, downtime, rework, inventory, labor). ROI ranges from 10x within 6 months. Most organizations recover the program investment from their first improvement initiative.

This is not theoretical training. 80% of the curriculum is applied directly to your operations, using your data, solving your challenges.

5-Day Curriculum

May 18–22, 2026 | 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM | De Palma, Shah Alam

Limited to 15 participants per cohort

Day 1Foundation of Kaizen ∧

Learning Focus: Kaizen philosophy, Champion leadership role, QCD framework, PDCA methodology, operational excellence mindset

MORNING: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Program Overview & Participant Introductions — Setting context and building group cohesion
  • Kaizen: Philosophy & Historical Context — Origins, evolution, and relevance to modern operations
  • The Role of a Kaizen Champion — Responsibilities, required competencies, organizational impact
  • QCD Framework — Quality, Cost, Delivery and their interconnection to profitability
  • The PDCA Cycle — Plan-Do-Check-Act as the engine of continuous improvement
AFTERNOON: 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Reactive vs. Proactive Mindset — From crisis management to prevention
  • Champion Competencies Framework — Technical, leadership, and change management skills
  • Operational Excellence Case Study — Analysis of successful improvement methodologies
  • PDCA Exercise: Applied Problem Solving — Small group practice on operational challenges
  • Personal Goal Setting — Individual commitment to learning and action
Day 2Baseline & Problem Identification ∧

Learning Focus: Current-state assessment, 8 Wastes, assessment methodology, data collection, 5S framework

MORNING: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Day 1 Recap & Reflection — Reinforcing PDCA and QCD concepts
  • The 8 Wastes (Muda) — Overproduction, Waiting, Transport, Processing, Inventory, Motion, Defects, Talent
  • Baseline Metrics & KPIs — Cycle time, defect rate, downtime, lead time, labor productivity
  • Assessment Methodology — Systematic approach to understanding current state
AFTERNOON: 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Process Assessment — Structured observation and data collection methods
  • Waste Identification Workshop — Recognize each type of waste in your process
  • 5S Framework — Sort, Set, Shine, Standardize, Sustain: organizing for efficiency
  • Data Collection Planning — Define process, select metrics, assign ownership, establish timeline
Day 3Root Cause Analysis & Solutions ∧

Learning Focus: Systematic root cause analysis, data-driven problem solving, countermeasure design, implementation planning

MORNING: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Root Cause Analysis Overview — Why symptoms must lead to systemic causes
  • 5 Why Analysis Method — Drilling down to true root causes through structured questioning
  • Fishbone Diagram Construction — Man, Machine, Method, Material, Environment framework
  • 7 Quality Control Tools — Check Sheet, Pareto, Histogram, Run Chart, Scatter Plot, Stratification, Control Chart
AFTERNOON: 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Data Analysis Workshop — Apply 7QC Tools to your baseline data, identify patterns
  • Countermeasure Development — Design specific, testable solutions to root causes
  • Quality Control Circles (QCC) — Employee-led problem-solving and suggestion systems
  • Implementation Plan Framework — Who, what, when, resources, success metrics
Day 4Implementation & Leadership ∧

Learning Focus: A3 reporting, mistake-proofing, visual management, standard work, change leadership

MORNING: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Implementation Mindset — From analysis to execution; starting small with pilot approaches
  • A3 Report Development — One-page improvement communication: background, analysis, solutions, plan, results
  • Poka Yoke (Mistake-Proofing) — Design processes to prevent errors before they occur
  • Jidoka (Autonomation) — Build in quality; processes/machines stop when abnormality detected
AFTERNOON: 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Visual Management Systems — Make status, problems, and targets visible at a glance
  • Andon System — Visual signals for abnormalities; production stops to address issues immediately
  • Standard Work Documentation — Best known method: sequence, timing, quality criteria
  • Leading Change — Communicate why, involve staff, train thoroughly, celebrate early wins
  • Pilot & Rollout Strategy — Start small, monitor daily, adjust quickly, scale to full deployment
Day 5Sustainability & Culture ∧

Learning Focus: Locking in improvements, preventing regression, building culture, KPI management, mentorship

MORNING: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Sustainability Strategy — Improvement is easy; sustaining it is the challenge
  • Systems & Habits for Sustainability — Standard work audits, visual metrics, accountability, recognition
  • Preventing Regression — Monthly reviews, quick-fix protocols, training refreshes, next-phase planning
  • Motivation & Engagement — Clear purpose, early wins, autonomy, growth opportunities
  • Managing Resistance — Address fear, habit, effort, ownership barriers with empathy and communication
AFTERNOON: 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • KPI Tracking & Dashboards — Quality, Cost, Delivery metrics over 90+ days
  • Business Impact Calculation — RM savings, ROI, payback period, link to business strategy
  • Mentorship & Coaching Framework — Identify potential, coach through PDCA, develop next leaders
  • Capstone Presentations — Each participant presents A3 Report, peer feedback, celebration
  • 90-Day Action Plan Review — Coaching schedule, email support, alumni network, next steps
  • Closing Ceremony — Recap, commitment, thank you, networking

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Lean or manufacturing experience? ∧
No. This program is self-contained and designed for operations professionals at any experience level. If you've done Lean before, this deepens that knowledge. If you haven't, this is a complete introduction. We assume only that you understand your process.
We tried improvements before. Nothing lasted. Why would this be different? ∧
Most improvements fail during the 6 months after training—not because the solution is flawed, but because follow-up is missing. This program includes 90 days of structured coaching to ensure improvements lock in. You get a sustainability plan on Day 5 and support throughout implementation.
Is this customized to our industry? ∧
Yes. 80% of the program is applied directly to YOUR process, YOUR challenges, and YOUR data. Whether you're automotive, electronics, food, pharma, or process manufacturing, we adapt examples and case studies. The assessment is on your operations.
What happens after the 5 days? ∧
You receive 90 days of structured support: 6 bi-weekly online coaching sessions (1 hour each), 24-hour email response, phone consultations as needed, and alumni network access. We track implementation milestones, refine your KPI dashboard, and help scale improvements across your operation.
How do we measure ROI? ∧
We track QCD metrics: defect rate, scrap %, cost per unit, lead time, on-time delivery %. You create a KPI dashboard on Day 5 with baseline, targets, and RM impact. Most companies see measurable savings within 90 days. Average ROI: 10x.
Who should attend? ∧
Manufacturing supervisors, engineers, operations managers, plant leaders, quality managers. Anyone responsible for process improvement or who directly manages teams on the floor. Mixed cohorts (different industries, functions) are ideal—peer learning is powerful. We limit to 15 participants for hands-on engagement.
Can you run this at our facility? ∧
Yes, for larger groups (8+). In-house delivery at your facility. Contact us to discuss dates, location, and customization options.
