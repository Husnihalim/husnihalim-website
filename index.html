About

Factory Floor Experience, Not Textbook Theory

Mohd Husni Bin Abd Halim is a HRDC-accredited trainer and EFESO-certified Process Kaizen Engineer — a credential obtained from EFESO Consulting in Milan, Italy, one of the world's premier manufacturing consultancies. He is also a Malaysia Productivity Corporation (MPC) QE5.0 External Auditor.

With a BSc in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Arizona (2003) and over 16 years in heavy manufacturing — including a decade-long tenure at Prysmian, the world's largest power cables company — Husni has led improvement projects across Malaysia, Italy, Brazil, China, Indonesia, and Norway.

He currently serves as Lead Consultant for TERAJU's Pritech 4.0 initiative, overseeing OEE system implementation across 30 Bumiputera factories, and as Lead Trainer for MARii's Automotive IPC Program, having trained 330+ engineers at UPM and PESDC.

His approach is grounded in factory-floor reality — not theory. Every program he delivers is built around the actual challenges Malaysian manufacturers face: resource constraints, cultural transitions, and building improvements that stick long after the engagement ends.

5S & Visual Management

Root Cause Analysis & Problem Solving

Production Leadership Coaching
