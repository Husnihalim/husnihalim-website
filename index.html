About

Lean Manufacturing Expertise for Malaysian Industry

With over a decade of hands-on experience in manufacturing operations, I work directly with Malaysian SMEs and larger facilities to implement lean manufacturing and Kaizen methodologies that actually stick.

Rather than textbook theory, my approach is grounded in factory-floor reality. I've worked through the challenges that Malaysian manufacturers face—resource constraints, cultural transitions, and sustainability beyond the initial event.

My training and consulting programs focus on building internal capability so your teams can continue improving long after the engagement ends.
