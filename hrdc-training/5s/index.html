01Programme Overview

What This Programme Covers

5S fails for one reason: it is treated as a cleaning programme. Factories run a 5S event, clean up the floor, take photographs, and three months later everything looks exactly as it did before. This program builds 5S as a management system — not an event. Participants learn all five pillars correctly, design a workable audit system for their facility, and most importantly, understand the management routines that make 5S self-sustaining. Hands-on red-tagging and zone assignment exercises happen on the actual factory floor.

Duration
1 Day (8 Hours)

Who Should Attend

02Programme Content

Programme Modules

M1

Module 1: 5S Philosophy

  • Why 5S exists — the management system it enables
  • 5S vs housekeeping — a critical distinction
  • The connection between 5S, safety, quality, and OEE
  • What world-class 5S looks like (photo tour)
M2

Module 2: Sort (Seiri)

  • Red-tag methodology — what stays, what goes
  • Red-tag event planning and execution
  • Disposal decision criteria for your facility
  • Hands-on red-tag exercise in selected area
M3

Module 3: Set in Order (Seiton)

  • A place for everything, everything in its place
  • Shadow boards, floor marking, label standards
  • Ergonomic placement for safety and efficiency
  • Zone assignment and ownership mapping
M4

Module 4: Shine (Seiso)

  • Cleaning as inspection — finding defects early
  • Cleaning schedule design
  • Equipment baseline cleaning standards
  • Inspection points identification
M5

Module 5: Standardise & Sustain (Seiketsu + Shitsuke)

  • 5S standard documentation: photos, checklists, zone maps
  • Audit scoring system design (customised for your facility)
  • Leader standard work for 5S compliance
  • Recognition systems and escalation paths
M6

Module 6: 5S Audit & Action Planning

  • Teams complete a scored 5S audit of their area
  • Gap analysis and prioritised action list
  • 30-day 5S implementation plan per zone
