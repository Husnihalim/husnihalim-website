Most factory teams jump to solutions before they have properly defined the problem — and the same issues recur month after month. This program builds systematic root-cause analysis capability using 8D, 5-Why, Fishbone Diagrams, Pareto Analysis, and A3 thinking. Participants work on real problems from their own production environment throughout the training — not textbook examples. By the end, each participant has a completed 8D or A3 report addressing an actual live issue.

01Programme Details

What You'll Learn

Who This Programme Is For

  • Manufacturing and Process Engineers responsible for recurring quality or production problems
  • Quality Assurance Engineers writing 8D reports for customer complaints
  • Production Supervisors who need to resolve shop-floor issues systematically
  • Maintenance Engineers investigating equipment failures and defects
  • Cross-functional improvement teams working on complex, multi-department problems

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply the 8D (8-Disciplines) framework to live production and quality problems
  • Use A3 thinking to structure problem solving communication and decision-making
  • Conduct effective root-cause analysis using 5-Why and Ishikawa (Fishbone) diagrams
  • Prioritize problems and identify focus areas using Pareto Analysis
  • Write clear, complete problem solving reports for internal and customer use
02Programme Schedule

What We Cover

Content is customized around your industry, equipment, and specific challenges during a pre-training needs analysis call.

Day 1

Foundations & Diagnosis

  • Why problems recur: the failure modes of unstructured problem solving
  • The 8D framework: all 8 disciplines explained with manufacturing examples
  • D1–D3: forming the team, defining the problem, implementing containment
  • D4: root cause analysis deep-dive — 5-Why, Fishbone/Ishikawa, and Is/Is-Not analysis
  • D5: selecting permanent corrective actions — criteria and decision methods
  • Workshop: participants begin an 8D report on a real problem from their workplace
Day 2

Implementation & Action Planning

  • D6–8: implementing, validating, and preventing recurrence — and recognizing the team
  • Introduction to A3 thinking: problem solving on a single page for visual management
  • Pareto analysis: data-driven prioritization of quality defects and production losses
  • Common 8D and A3 pitfalls: vague problem statements, shallow root causes, weak countermeasures
  • Writing effective 8D reports for customer submission: structure, language, and evidence
  • Workshop: participants complete and present their 8D or A3 on a real problem

05Common Questions

Before You Book

Can participants bring real problems to work on during the training?
Yes — this is specifically encouraged. The workshop component of both days is designed for participants to work on real, live problems from their own production environment. Bring your current recurring defects, equipment failures, or customer complaints. The training is more valuable when applied to real problems immediately.
Is 8D suitable for all types of manufacturing problems?
8D works best for customer-complaint-level problems or significant recurring defects that require a structured, documented corrective action. For simpler day-to-day issues, 5-Why or A3 may be more appropriate. The program covers how to choose the right tool for the right problem.
How is A3 different from 8D?
A3 is a visual, single-page problem solving story used for internal management and decision-making — focused on clarity and alignment. 8D is a more formal, step-by-step corrective action process often used for customer-facing quality complaints. Both are covered in this program.
What industries is this training most relevant for?
Any manufacturing environment where problems recur: automotive, electronics, semiconductor, FMCG, aerospace, medical devices. The tools are industry-agnostic; the case studies and workshop problems are tailored to your sector.
Is this program 100% HRDC claimable?
Yes. Delivered by an HRDC Accredited Trainer. Fully claimable under SBL-Khas with complete documentation provided for your grant application.
