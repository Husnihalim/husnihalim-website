    01 Industry Challenges

    What Makes Electronics & Semiconductor Manufacturing Difficult

    Electronics and semiconductor factories operate under pressures that amplify every operational inefficiency — high capital equipment costs, yield sensitivity, and unforgiving customer quality requirements.

    📊
    Equipment OEE Measurement Gaps
    High-value equipment in electronics factories is often tracked for uptime — but speed losses and quality losses remain invisible. Without full OEE visibility, the biggest losses go unfixed.
    🎯
    Yield Variation and Defect Escape
    Defect rates that are acceptable in other industries are catastrophic in electronics. A single-digit PPM escape to a customer represents a quality failure. Finding variation sources requires structured root cause methodology.
    🔧
    Preventive Maintenance Gaps
    Expensive equipment breaks down at the worst moments. TPM implementation — planned maintenance, autonomous maintenance by operators, and predictive routines — reduces unplanned downtime significantly.
    📦
    Material Flow and WIP Accumulation
    Work-in-progress piling up between processes hides flow problems and creates quality risks. Value stream mapping reveals where flow breaks down and which steps are true bottlenecks.
    🏗️
    ESD and Contamination Control
    5S in electronics factories must account for ESD sensitivity, cleanroom discipline, and component handling standards — it is not just organisation, it is contamination prevention and quality assurance.
    📈
    Capacity Scaling Without Chaos
    Customer demand surges create pressure to scale fast. Without standard work and documented processes, scaling up introduces quality variation. Process stability must precede volume ramp.

    03 Why It Matters

    In Electronics, Equipment Losses Are Expensive Losses

    High-value semiconductor equipment sitting at 72% OEE when it could run at 85% is not a maintenance problem — it is a structured improvement problem. Every percentage point of OEE improvement in a high-throughput electronics line translates directly into revenue. The tools to get there exist. The discipline to sustain them is what I build.
