# Kaizen Champion Marketing Launch Assets

## Core Positioning

**Build the person who owns improvement.**

The Kaizen Champion Development Program is a 5-day practical program for Malaysian manufacturing teams that need internal people who can take ownership of workplace problems, lead structured Kaizen action, delegate ownership to others, and sustain improvement habit after training.

Use this campaign line:

**Not just attend. Produce. Apply. Review.**

Avoid positioning this as an external fix. The message is internal ownership, daily improvement habit, and practical workplace output.

## Program Facts

- Program: Kaizen Champion Development Program
- Dates: Confirmed directly with interested companies
- Format: Public cohort or in-house delivery
- Fee: RM 6,500 per participant
- Status: HRDC claimable for eligible Malaysian companies
- Cohort: limited intake
- Support: 90-day coaching plus 1 site visit progress assessment

## Program Output Guarantee

Every participant who completes the 5-day program will leave with:

- Completed A3 improvement report
- Practical Kaizen toolkit
- KPI tracking structure
- 90-day implementation roadmap
- 6 coaching sessions
- 1 site visit to assess progress on the participant's Kaizen initiative

This is a program-output guarantee. It does not promise a fixed financial saving or productivity percentage.
